# Visual assets

The hero and six category photos are AI-generated illustrations created for this prototype. They do not document real donations or real organizations. Donation pages label category imagery as illustrative.

The three community avatar SVGs and the favicon were drawn for the prototype. They are decorative illustrations, not photographs of registered users.

No external image host is required at runtime. Self-hosted DM Sans and Manrope font files, with their SIL Open Font License texts, are in `static/fonts/`.
