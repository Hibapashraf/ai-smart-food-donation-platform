What goes into a match?

A transparent, weighted recommendation model. Five signals. Up to 100 points.

{% for points,label,text in [(30,'Location','Closer charities score higher within their collection radius.'),(25,'Food compatibility','The category must be one the organization needs.'),(20,'Quantity','Similar quantities in the same unit get more points.'),(10,'Urgency','Time-sensitive food receives additional priority.'),(15,'Expiry suitability','Enough time for coordination, travel, and safe pickup.')] %}

Safety and fit come first.Expired food, incompatible dietary types, unneeded categories, and locations outside the collection radius are excluded. We estimate travel at 20 km/h plus 30 minutes of coordination and require enough time before expiry. Haversine distance works without a Maps API key. This is a simple content-based recommender, not a trained predictive model or food safety certification.
