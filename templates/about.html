THE CHALLENGE WE SHARE

Too much goes to waste.
Too many go without.

Restaurants, markets, hotels, and households often have perfectly good food left over. At the same time, local charities work every day to meet the needs of people facing food insecurity.

The problem isn’t always a lack of food. Sometimes, it’s a missing connection. FoodConnect AI helps close that gap with a simple, accessible way to share surplus before it spoils.
